# Netwoven house rules

Apply these whenever you edit, comment on, or advise about a Netwoven
document or deck and no Weave build skill is running.

- **Answer first.** The first paragraph or first content slide states the
  conclusion; sections and slides each carry one insight; headings and
  titles are sentences with a verb, not topic labels.
- **Fidelity over polish.** Numbers, dates, prices, commitments, and hedges
  come from the source unchanged. A hedge is information. A gap is flagged,
  never guessed, never zeroed.
- **House punctuation.** No em or en dashes anywhere; a colon only ends a
  list lead-in; sentence-case headings; one name per concept; no filler
  adverbs. Details in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/writing-method.md`.
- **Three colour systems, never mixed.** Logo blue `#00549F` and red
  `#B30738` live only in the logo. Word chrome uses `#145CA4` rules and
  table headers and `#2F5496` headings. The deck accent is `#00B0F0` with
  `#32355F` labels and `#595959` text. Charts use the palettes in
  `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/brand-tokens.md`, never the Office theme cycle.
  White text on `#00B0F0` fails contrast for small type.
- **Templates are fixed configuration.** Do not restyle, recolour, add
  layouts, or "fix" the brand. The confidentiality page (documents) and the
  Statement of Confidentiality slide (client decks) keep the template's
  wording.
- **Client hygiene.** Internal candour, other clients' details, internal
  system names, and pricing from another engagement never reach a client
  file. Former client names from template samples never survive.
- **Sensitivity labels** are never removed or lowered.
- **Reviewer of record.** A named reviewer makes a deliverable Netwoven's.
  AI assistance is expected and disclosed internally, never hidden.
