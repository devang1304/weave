# Netwoven Weave

Weave turns your notes, transcripts, estimates, and drafts into client-ready
Netwoven deliverables on the 2026 templates, and checks any deliverable
before it goes out. It works in Cowork, in Claude Code, and in Microsoft
Copilot Cowork.

## Try these sentences

- "Turn these workshop notes into a client-ready assessment for Contoso."
- "Make a 10-slide QBR deck from this status email for Fabrikam."
- "Draft the SOW for the SharePoint migration from this estimate."
- "Review this deck before I send it."

Weave picks the right skill from what you say. You can also call one
directly: `/weave:document`, `/weave:deck`, `/weave:sow`, `/weave:review`,
`/weave:setup`, `/weave:feedback`.

## What you get

| You ask for | You get | Template |
|---|---|---|
| A document for a client or an internal one | Word document (or short Markdown for working notes) | Netwoven 2026 document template |
| Slides, a deck, a readout, a QBR | PowerPoint deck with a confidentiality slide and Netwoven closers | Netwoven 2026 presentation template |
| A SOW, scope with fees, rate card | Signable statement of work, Milestone or T&M | Netwoven 2026 SOW templates |
| A check before sending | Verdict, score, the story your titles tell, and a fix list | Any Netwoven .docx or .pptx |

Every build ends with a short list in chat: **Check before sending**,
**I assumed**, **I left out**, **Open decisions**, and the exact place the
file was saved. Weave never invents numbers, dates, or commitments; gaps are
flagged, not filled.

## First time

Run `/weave:setup`. It checks the device, installs what Weave needs, records
your name and role for revision pages, and can save client profiles so you
stop retyping legal names. It also shows the usage-data note and how to turn
that off, and, when the plugin team has set up Netwoven's usage list, signs
you in to it once with a Microsoft device code.

## Weave learns your habits

When you change a Weave-built file by hand and later ask Weave to read it
back, it notes the difference. A change it sees three times (a word you
always replace, a section you always remove) becomes a preference the build
skills apply. Preferences stay on your device; `weave.py memory show` lists
them and `weave.py memory forget KEY` drops one.

## Editing later

Attach a file Weave built and say what to change ("swap the client name",
"add a risk about licensing", "make slide 4 a chart"). Weave reads the plan
it stored inside the file, applies the change, rebuilds, and re-checks. If
you edited the file by hand in Office first, your edits win.

## Where files land

Weave names files `<Client>_<Type>_<YYYY-MM-DD>_v<n>` and tells you the
full path. In Cowork the file also appears in the session's output list; in
Microsoft Copilot Cowork it appears in your OneDrive `Cowork` folder.

## Rules Weave follows

- Templates are used exactly as designed; nothing is restyled.
- The confidentiality page and slide keep the template's wording.
- Sensitivity labels are never removed or lowered.
- Internal candour, other clients' details, and credentials never reach a
  client file; see `references/guardrails.md`.
- A named reviewer makes a deliverable Netwoven's. Using Weave is expected,
  disclosed internally, and never held against you.
